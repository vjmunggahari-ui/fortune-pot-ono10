One kind, funny fortune for a houseplant in 120 characters or fewer, written by `main.ts` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

> You will lean so far toward the morning light that your keeper will turn your pot, and you will lean again.

The plant leans toward the morning light, the keeper turns the pot to correct it, and the plant leans right back — a small, stubborn, affectionate loop between a houseplant and its keeper, told as a prediction.
